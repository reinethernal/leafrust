LeafRust is a free and open-source Android app that analyzes a photo of a plant leaf on your device.

• Take a photo or pick from the gallery
• Align the leaf in a frame (pan, zoom, rotate)
• Estimate disease and damage percentage with an on-device TensorFlow Lite model
• Read symptoms and treatment tips from an offline plant/disease knowledge base
• Keep a local history of inspections and share results via the Android share sheet

No accounts, ads, or analytics. Photos stay on your phone unless you share them. Internet access is only used to optionally download or refresh the model.
